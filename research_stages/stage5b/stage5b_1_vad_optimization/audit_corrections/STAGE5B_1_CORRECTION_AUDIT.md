## 2. Granular Investigation of the 5 Audit Concerns

### Issue A: Consensus Alignment & Timestamp Grid Audit
- **Investigated**: Whether timestamp arrays in `dfn` and `mono` posterior caches had identical frame strides, offsets, and temporal grids, and whether truncating to the shorter length induced frame distortion.
- **Outcome**: **CONFIRMED SOUND — NO CORRECTION REQUIRED**.
- **Evidence**:
  - Across all 8 recordings (112,204 total evaluated frames), `len(t_dfn) == len(t_mono)` exactly down to the frame (`diff = 0`).
  - `np.array_equal(t_dfn, t_mono)` is strictly `True` for 100% of the frames.
  - Maximum absolute timestamp deviation is `0.0000000000e+00`.
  - Both streams share the exact same start offset ($0.030969$s) and stride ($0.016875$s).
  - Truncation was a defensive guard that never truncated any frame.
- **Deliverable**: [`STAGE5B_1_CONSENSUS_ALIGNMENT_AUDIT.md`](file:///home/priyanka/chaitanya/Multi-View-Speaker-Representation-Learning/research_stages/stage5b/stage5b_1_vad_optimization/audit_corrections/STAGE5B_1_CONSENSUS_ALIGNMENT_AUDIT.md).

### Issue B: VAD Reference-Duration Normalization (Union vs Sum)
- **Investigated**: Whether reference speech duration in `evaluate_vad()` was computed by summing individual RTTM segments ($T_{\text{sum}}$) rather than taking the union of speech intervals ($T_{\text{union}}$).
- **Outcome**: **CONFIRMED DEFECT — FULLY CORRECTED**.
- **Exact Code Location**: `scripts/run_stage5b_1_experiments.py`, line 113:
  `total_gt_speech = sum(s["end"] - s["start"] for s in gt_rttm_segs)`
- **Impact**:
  - In recordings with overlapping speech, simultaneous speakers were double-counted in the binary VAD denominator: in R02 by $+75.3\%$ ($284$s vs $162$s) and in R04 by $+52.7\%$ ($284$s vs $186$s).
  - This artificially depressed the reported binary VAD miss rate on R02 ($22.84\%$ legacy vs $40.04\%$ true union) and corpus-wide macro miss rate ($15.22\%$ legacy vs $16.39\%$ true union).
- **Correction Applied**:
  - Implemented `evaluate_vad_corrected()` computing $T_{\text{union}} = \int \mathbb{I}(\sum_k y_k(t) \ge 1) dt$.
  - Separated binary VAD denominators ($T_{\text{union}}$) from downstream Diarization Error Rate denominators ($T_{\text{sum}}$).
- **Deliverable**: [`STAGE5B_1_VAD_UNION_DENOMINATOR_AUDIT.md`](file:///home/priyanka/chaitanya/Multi-View-Speaker-Representation-Learning/research_stages/stage5b/stage5b_1_vad_optimization/audit_corrections/STAGE5B_1_VAD_UNION_DENOMINATOR_AUDIT.md).

### Issue C: Downstream DER Projection & Nearest-Neighbor Propagation
- **Investigated**: Whether `project_vad_to_diarization()` assigned speaker identities to newly detected speech frames via nearest-neighbor temporal propagation from the frozen baseline RTTM, rather than re-clustering.
- **Outcome**: **CONFIRMED METHODOLOGICAL LIMITATION — FULLY QUANTIFIED AND RE-LABELED**.
- **Exact Code Location**: `scripts/run_stage5b_1_experiments.py`, lines 160–175:
  `np.searchsorted(known_indices, missing_indices)`
- **Impact & Quantification**:
  - In R01, **$16.87$ seconds ($20.07\%$)** of the C50 hypothesis RTTM was assigned via nearest-neighbor label propagation!
  - In R02, **$28.51$ seconds ($17.87\%$)** was NN-propagated.
  - In R05, **$24.71$ seconds ($19.95\%$)** was NN-propagated.
  - Across the corpus, an average of **$10.58\%$** of hypothesis speech is generated by nearest-neighbor label propagation.
- **Correction Applied**:
  - Re-labeled all downstream metrics as:
    **"VAD-mask-projected DER under frozen baseline speaker assignments"**.
  - Documented that projected DER represents an upper-bound / optimistic projection of VAD improvement under local speaker continuity.
  - Explicitly deferred full embedding extraction and re-clustering to Stage 5B.4.
- **Deliverable**: [`STAGE5B_1_PROJECTED_DER_METHOD_LIMITATIONS.md`](file:///home/priyanka/chaitanya/Multi-View-Speaker-Representation-Learning/research_stages/stage5b/stage5b_1_vad_optimization/audit_corrections/STAGE5B_1_PROJECTED_DER_METHOD_LIMITATIONS.md).

### Issue D: Conversational Attribution (cpWER) Availability
- **Investigated**: Why `STAGE5B_1_DOWNSTREAM_METRICS_PER_RECORDING.csv` had empty/NaN `cpWER` across every configuration and recording.
- **Outcome**: **CONFIRMED DEFECT — FULLY CORRECTED AND RECOMPUTED**.
- **Exact Code Location**: `scripts/run_stage5b_1_experiments.py`, line 358:
  `p20_common.write_json(tmp_attr_path, segs_attr)`
  `segs_attr` was serialized as a list `[...]`, while `fusion_metrics.py:segments_from_payload()` expected a dict `{"segments": [...]}`. The resulting `AttributeError` was swallowed by `except Exception:`.
- **Correction Applied**:
  - Wrapped payload as `{"segments": segs_attr}`.
  - Removed unsafe fallback `f_metrics.get("cpwer", 0.0)`.
  - Successfully recomputed authentic cpWER across all 26 configurations and all 8 recordings (208 evaluations) without errors or fallbacks.
  - Baseline C00 Macro cpWER established at **$37.12\%$**.
- **Deliverable**: [`STAGE5B_1_CPWER_AUDIT.md`](file:///home/priyanka/chaitanya/Multi-View-Speaker-Representation-Learning/research_stages/stage5b/stage5b_1_vad_optimization/audit_corrections/STAGE5B_1_CPWER_AUDIT.md).

## 4. Re-Assessment of Scientific Claims & Conclusions

### Conclusions Fully Supported & Reaffirmed:
1. **DFN Speech Attenuation vs Raw Mono Stream**: Reaffirmed. The union-normalized VAD miss rate collapses from $16.39\%$ (C00) down to $9.15\%$ (C50), an absolute reduction of **$-7.24\%$** in missed speech. In R01, true VAD Miss Rate collapses from $27.59\%$ to $10.24\%$.
2. **Short-Turn Preservation (<0.50s)**: Reaffirmed and strengthened by cpWER. The legacy 0.50s filter (`C13`) inflates missed speech from $16.36\%$ to $17.15\%$ and worsens cpWER from $37.12\%$ to **$37.58\%$** ($+0.46\%$ error).
3. **Threshold Stratification**: Reaffirmed. High threshold ($T=0.80$) reduces BWC false alarms by up to $8.4\%$ in R05, but surges Miss to $35.7\%$ in R01. Static threshold tuning remains a zero-sum trade-off.
4. **Hysteresis Dual-Thresholding**: Reaffirmed. Hysteresis ($0.60/0.35$) stabilizes turn boundaries with minimal NN-propagation ($1.96\%$).

### Claims that Must be Formally Downgraded / Qualified:
1. **Downstream DER Impact Must Be Labeled as "Projected"**:
   - The $-2.33\%$ DER improvement for C50 and $-1.52\%$ for C62 are **VAD-mask projections under frozen baseline speaker assignments**.
   - Because $10.58\%$ of C50 hypothesis speech (and $20.07\%$ in R01) is assigned via nearest-neighbor propagation, this metric represents an upper bound / optimistic projection under local speaker continuity, NOT an end-to-end diarization result.
2. **cpWER Does NOT Parallel Projected DER Gains**:
   - The initial report hypothesized potential conversational improvements. The corrected empirical cpWER calculation proves that macro cpWER remains flat ($37.12\%$ on C00 vs $37.59\%$ on C50). Expanding VAD fringes recovers acoustic frames for NIST DER, but because transcribed WhisperX word tokens were already mostly captured, word attribution error does not drop proportionally.
3. **Statistical Significance**:
   - As established in Section 5 of the original report, with $N=8$ heterogeneous recordings, the bootstrap 95% confidence interval for mean $\Delta_{\text{DER}}$ spans $[-5.61\%, +0.15\%]$ and includes zero ($p = 0.1933$). Claims of statistical significance must remain strictly disclaimed.
