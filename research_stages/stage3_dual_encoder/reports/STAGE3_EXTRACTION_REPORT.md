### Key Structural Observations from Stage 3 Extraction (`VERIFIED`):
1. **Opposite Pre-Normalization L2 Norm Behavior Across Scales**:
   - As the temporal window shortens from `1.5s` (Scale 0) to `0.5s` (Scale 4), **ResNet293-LM's pre-normalization L2 norm systematically decreases** (e.g., on `R01_sara_sharif`, from `1.5107` at `1.5s` down to `1.1566` at `0.5s`) with a wide dynamic range (`std ~ 0.37–0.64`), behaving like a classic quality-sensitive embedding magnitude indicator.
   - Conversely, **TitaNet-Large's pre-normalization L2 norm slightly increases** as windows shorten (`0.7917` at `1.5s` to `0.9316` at `0.5s`) and decreases under `DeepFilterNet3` (`0.9316` on `16k_mono` vs. `0.8378` on `dfn`).
2. **Loudness Normalization Invariance**:
   - Comparing `16k_mono` vs. `normalized` (`-20 LUFS`), both encoders yield nearly identical L2 norms (`diff < 0.01`), because TitaNet applies per-feature variance normalization and ResNet293-LM applies utterance-level log-mel CMN (where a linear gain shift in time-domain amplitude becomes an additive constant in log-mel space that is subtracted out by CMN).
