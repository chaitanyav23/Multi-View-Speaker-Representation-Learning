## A. Executive Conclusion

1. **What Was Methodologically Flawed in Stage 4**:
   - **Cross-Recording Pooled Verification (`pooled_all_pairs`, `32,131` pairs) Was Invalid**: All speaker IDs in `ground_truth/*.txt` are anonymous recording-local labels (`Speaker_0..Speaker_16`, `Speaker_all`) namespaced as `rec_id::Speaker_X`. Zero (`0`) verified cross-recording positive speaker pairs exist. Stage 4's `pooled_all_pairs` combined `2,828` within-recording positive pairs with `6,989` within-recording negative pairs + `22,314` cross-recording negative pairs, confounding speaker verification with session/recording acoustic classification.
   - **Pairwise Verification Thresholds & Z-Score Normalization Used In-Sample Statistics**: In Stage 4, EER thresholds ($\tau_T, \tau_R$), Z-score calibration ($\mu, \sigma$), and the $2\times 2$ binary error contingency table were computed in-sample on the evaluation pairs rather than frozen on training recordings under Leave-One-Recording-Out (LORO).
   - **Reliability Indicator Evaluation Had Centroid Self-Inclusion & Overlap Contamination**: Stage 4 computed speaker centroids using all clean subsegments of speaker $s$ in a recording (including query subsegment $i$ itself and adjacent 50%-overlapping subsegments from the same turn), evaluated `5,290` subsegments that included `703` `OVERLAP::` and `353` `PARTIAL_BOUNDARY::` clips alongside `4,234` clean clips, and applied in-sample sign rectification `max(auroc, 1 - auroc)`. Additionally, `overlap_prob_mean` was identically `0.0` due to `step03_vad.py` collapsing `pyannote/segmentation`'s 3-speaker output `[frames, 3]` to `[frames, 1]` via `np.max`.
   - **LORO Procrustes & LORO Gate Used Unweighted Subsegment Stacking**: Although both genuinely excluded the held-out recording from parameter fitting, both concatenated training subsegments without inverse-recording-size weights ($1/N_r$), allowing `R06_rich_karen` to dominate ~35% of the training loss (and `76.43%` of within-recording turn pairs).
   - **Report Text Had Three Stale Draft Numbers**: Prose in `STAGE4_DECISION_GATE_REPORT.md` cited `388` clean turns (`4,061/5,756` pairs) instead of the `254` VAD-covered clean turns (`2,828/6,989` pairs) in `stage4_master_results.json`; cited `40,386` cached subsegments instead of the `15,994` per condition (`47,982` across 3 conditions; `95,964` embedding vectors) on disk; and cited `-20 LUFS` instead of the `loudnorm=I=-16:TP=-1.5:LRA=11` (`-16 LUFS`) command actually executed in `run_stage1_prep.py` line 471.

2. **What Remains Scientifically Valid After Strict Correction**:
   - **Global Representational Similarity alongside Genuine Error Diversity**: Linear CKA between frozen TitaNet-Large and ResNet293-LM is `0.6317` on all `5,622` Scale-4 subsegments (`0.6601` on `4,234` clean subsegments). Under **strictly frozen LORO thresholds** on the `9,817` within-recording turn pairs ([loro_fusion_comparison.csv](file:///home/priyanka/chaitanya/Multi-View-Speaker-Representation-Learning/research_stages/stage4_1/loro_fusion_comparison.csv)), out-of-fold error Jaccard is **`0.3667`** on `16k_mono` (`63.33%` of errors are single-encoder complementary errors: $n_{10}=726$ TitaNet-only errors, $n_{01}=1,364$ ResNet293-only errors, $n_{11}=1,210$ shared errors) and **`0.4428`** on `dfn` (`55.72%` complementary errors).
   - **Vector-Space Linear Alignment (Procrustes) Fails Out-of-Fold Across All 4 Corrected Variants**: Even with exact equal-recording weights ($1/7$ per training recording), clean-only subsegment fitting, or mean centering ([corrected_loro_results.csv](file:///home/priyanka/chaitanya/Multi-View-Speaker-Representation-Learning/research_stages/stage4_1/corrected_loro_results.csv)), held-out LORO Procrustes $R^2$ is **strictly negative on every fold (`-0.33` to `-0.54` mean $R^2$)**. Affinity/score-space combination is strictly superior to feature-space linear projection.
   - **Top 3 Reliability Indicators Survive Strict Turn-Disjoint Leave-One-Turn-Out (LOTO) & Split-Enrollment Protocols**: When speaker prototypes are computed strictly from **other turns ($t' \neq t$)** of the same speaker with zero self-inclusion and zero overlap contamination ([reliability_loro_metrics.csv](file:///home/priyanka/chaitanya/Multi-View-Speaker-Representation-Learning/research_stages/stage4_1/reliability_loro_metrics.csv)), **`resnet293_l2_norm`**, **`dfn_noise_reduction_db`**, and **`vad_conf_mean`** retain strong, recording-consistent predictive power:
     - On `2,771` clean LOTO subsegments (`16k_mono`): `resnet293_l2_norm` achieves $\rho(\text{Res margin}) = \mathbf{+0.6952}$, Held-Out Correctness AUROC = **`0.8419`**, and Held-Out "ResNet Wins Margin" AUROC = **`0.6544`** (`0.7024` on LOTO turns; `0.7305` on split-enrollment turns).

   - **Preprocessing Asymmetry Is Statistically Significant**: Under recording-clustered bootstrap (`B = 2,000`), ResNet293-LM on raw `16k_mono` (`10_resnet293_mono_nmesc`, Macro DER `45.98%`, cpWER `36.44%`) significantly outperforms ResNet293-LM on `dfn` (`2_resnet293_nmesc`, Macro DER `47.60%`, cpWER `39.96%`) by **`-1.63%` Macro DER** (`95% CI [-2.66%, -0.68%]`, winning `7/8` recordings), whereas TitaNet-Large requires `dfn` on `R01_sara_sharif` to avoid single-speaker collapse.

Only the hybrid preprocessing fusion (`11_hybrid_titaDFN_resMONO_zscore`, Macro cpWER **`37.05%`**, $\Delta = -1.02\%$, `95% CI [-2.13%, -0.12%]`) and the gate-vs-equal-fusion comparison on cpWER ($\Delta = -0.62\%$, `95% CI [-1.69%, -0.03%]`) exclude zero at the 95% level.

## D. Scientific Interpretation

1. **Complementarity Exists in Score/Affinity Space, Not Linear Vector Space**:
   - Frozen TitaNet-Large (1D depthwise-separable ConvNet trained on cleanly segmented VoxCeleb/RIR speech, enhanced here with DeepFilterNet) and WeSpeaker ResNet293-LM (2D residual network with large-margin fine-tuning, strongest here on unenhanced `16k_mono`) make **genuinely different verification errors (`63.3%` complementary errors on `16k_mono`, `55.7%` on `dfn` under frozen LORO thresholds)**.
   - However, their raw embedding spaces cannot be linearly rotated into one another across recordings (`LORO Procrustes R^2 < -0.32` across all variants). Combining them in **cosine affinity space** preserves both encoders' geometric structure without overfitting.
2. **Reliability Indicators Are Real, Leak-Free Predictors of Embedding Degradation**:
   - The Stage 4.1 LOTO audit proves that the predictive power of `resnet293_l2_norm` (`Held-Out AUROC = 0.8419`), `dfn_noise_reduction_db` (`0.7821`), `vad_conf_mean` (`0.7499`), and `resnet293_cross_scale_cos` (`0.7157`) is **not an artifact of centroid self-inclusion**.
   - Specifically, when `resnet293_l2_norm` is high and `dfn_noise_reduction_db` is moderate (clean or moderately noisy speech without aggressive spectral subtraction), ResNet293-LM is highly reliable; when `resnet293_l2_norm` collapses or `dfn_noise_reduction_db` is extreme, TitaNet-Large is more resilient.
3. **Why Downstream Diarization DER Shows Only Marginal Movement (`~0.16%–0.24%`)**:
   - Across the 8 BWC recordings (`46.22%` baseline Macro DER), **`37.74%` (`13.33%` False Alarm + `24.41%` Missed Speech) is locked in by the shared Path A VAD segmentation and overlapping speech**, leaving only **`8.48%` Speaker Confusion** that spectral clustering can ever modify.
   - Furthermore, NME-SC's discrete eigengap threshold (`max_rp_threshold=0.15`) quantizes continuous changes in the affinity matrix into discrete cluster counts $K$: on 7 of the 8 recordings (`R01, R02, R03, R04, R05, R07, R08`), all DFN affinity fusion methods (`3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 8b`) predict the **exact same number of speakers** (`[3, 3, 3, 3, 3, 1, 2]`), differing only on `R06_rich_karen` (`6` speakers for TitaNet and Gate vs `5` speakers for Equal/Z-score fusion and `8` speakers for ResNet293).

## F. Relevance to a Generative AI / Conversational Intelligence Portfolio

Even with the dataset's sample-size constraints ($N=8$ recordings), the completed Stages 0–4.1 investigation provides a high-value, production-grade foundation for **Operational Conversational Intelligence and LLM/GenAI Downstream Systems**:

1. **Speaker-Attributed ASR (`cpWER`, `saWER`, `SACC`) for Multi-Party LLM Summarization & RAG**:
   - Downstream LLMs summarizing body-worn camera incidents, parliamentary debates, or customer escalations fail catastrophically when speaker turns are merged (`single-speaker collapse`) or misattributed across roles (e.g., attributing a suspect's statement to the officer).
   - Our pipeline directly links acoustic affinity matrices to **Whisper ASR word-level speaker attribution (`cpWER`, `Micro/Macro-SACC`)**, demonstrating how condition-specific dual-encoder fusion (`TitaNet-DFN + ResNet293-Mono`) eliminates `R01`'s single-speaker collapse, reduces speaker-count MAE to `1.25`, and improves speaker-attributed word error rate (`cpWER`) from `38.07%` to `37.05%` (`36.44%` with `ResNet293-Mono`).
2. **Uncertainty & Reliability Estimation (`resnet293_l2_norm`, `dfn_noise_reduction_db`, `vad_conf_mean`) as Metadata for Agentic / LLM Pipelines**:
   - The leak-free finding that pre-norm embedding magnitude (`resnet293_l2_norm`, Held-Out AUROC `0.8419`) and enhancement suppression (`dfn_noise_reduction_db`, Held-Out AUROC `0.7821`) strongly predict speaker-attribution correctness enables **confidence-aware transcript formatting**: segments with low reliability scores can be flagged as `[SPEAKER_UNCERTAIN / LOW_ACOUSTIC_QUALITY]` when passed to an LLM prompt, preventing hallucinated speaker accountability in high-stakes legal/public-safety applications.
3. **Exemplary ML Research Rigor (Leakage Detection, LORO/LOTO Validation, Clustered Bootstrapping)**:
