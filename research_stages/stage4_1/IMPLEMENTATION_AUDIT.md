  - `normalized`: `ffmpeg -y -i <mono_wav> -af loudnorm=I=-16:TP=-1.5:LRA=11 -ar 16000 -ac 1 -acodec pcm_s16le <norm_wav>`

### 2.2 Stage 2 & Stage 3: VAD Subsegmentation, Dual-Encoder Extraction & Metadata
- **Subsegment Boundaries** ([`run_stage3_extraction.py#L415-L424`](file:///home/priyanka/chaitanya/Multi-View-Speaker-Representation-Learning/research_stages/stage3_dual_encoder/run_stage3_extraction.py#L415-L424)):
  - Reads the exact 5-scale subsegment manifests generated by NeMo on Path A (`dfn`) in Stage 2 (`subsegments_scale{0..4}.json`), yielding `1,840` (Scale 0: `1.5s`), `2,297` (Scale 1: `1.2s`), `2,761` (Scale 2: `1.0s`), `3,474` (Scale 3: `0.8s`), and `5,622` (Scale 4: `0.5s`) subsegments across `R01–R08` (`15,994` subsegments per audio condition).
- **Bug Found in `overlap_prob_mean`** ([`run_stage2_baseline.py#L108-L125`](file:///home/priyanka/chaitanya/Multi-View-Speaker-Representation-Learning/research_stages/stage2_baseline/run_stage2_baseline.py#L108-L125), [`step03_vad.py#L46-L52`](file:///home/priyanka/chaitanya/Multi-View-Speaker-Representation-Learning/phase20_standalone/scripts/step03_vad.py#L46-L52)):
  - In `step03_vad.py`, `pipeline._segmentation(file)` invokes `VoiceActivityDetection._segmentation`, which applies a `pre_aggregation_hook` (`np.max(scores, axis=-1, keepdims=True)`) that collapses the 3-speaker `PyanNet` output `[frames, 3]` to `[frames, 1]` before returning `probabilities`.
  - Consequently, in `run_stage2_baseline.py` line 121, `raw_data.shape[-1] >= 2` is always `False`, setting `p_overlap = np.zeros_like(p_speech)` (all zeros). Every subsegment in Stage 3 therefore has `overlap_prob_mean = 0.0`, explaining why `overlap_prob_mean` had `NaN` Spearman correlation and `0.5000` AUROC in Stage 4.
- **Ground-Truth Label Assignment on Subsegments** ([`run_stage3_extraction.py#L245-L280`](file:///home/priyanka/chaitanya/Multi-View-Speaker-Representation-Learning/research_stages/stage3_dual_encoder/run_stage3_extraction.py#L245-L280)):
  - Matches each subsegment `[st, en]` to positive-duration GT segments (`gt_label` = `Speaker_X` if single-speaker coverage $\ge 75\%$, else `OVERLAP::Speaker_X`, `PARTIAL_BOUNDARY::Speaker_X`, `AMBIGUOUS_SPEAKER_ALL`, or `NON_SPEECH_OR_ROUNDING_GAP`).
  - Note: `gt_label`, `gt_purity`, and `gt_is_overlap` are stored in `subsegments_metadata__scale<k>.json` strictly for evaluation stratification and supervised gate target construction; they do **not** enter the frozen TitaNet or ResNet293 embedding forward pass or unsupervised NME-SC affinity matrices (`Methods 1–6, 9–11`).
