## 2. Critical Analysis of Attention & Gating Architectures (`Phase G2`)

The base paper (Section 6.2) mentions *"shared latent representation space, lightweight self-attention, and reliability-aware fusion."* We must evaluate how self-attention vs. reliability-gated fusion behaves under the **actual data constraints** verified in Phase D (`312` positive-duration segments across 8 recordings, `25` speakers with $\ge 2$ segments):

### 2.1 Why Naive Multi-Head Self-Attention Over 2 Views Overfits (`VERIFIED` Constraint)
Suppose we project $\mathbf{e}_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{192}$ and $\mathbf{e}_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{256}$ to $\hat{\mathbf{e}}_1, \hat{\mathbf{e}}_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{d}$ ($d=256$) and apply a standard Transformer Multi-Head Self-Attention (MHSA) layer + FFN over the sequence of $M=2$ tokens $[\hat{\mathbf{e}}_1, \hat{\mathbf{e}}_2]$:
1. **Parameter Inflation:**
   - Linear projections: $192 \times 256 + 256 \times 256 = 114,688$ params.
   - 1-layer MHSA ($d=256$): $4 \times 256^2 = 262,144$ params.
   - FFN ($256 \to 512 \to 256$): $262,144$ params.
   - **Total:** **$\sim 640,000$ trainable parameters**!
2. **Data-to-Parameter Mismatch:**
   - Training $640,000$ parameters on **25 speakers / 312 segments** (ratio of **$> 2,000$ parameters per training segment**) is guaranteed to memorize recording-specific channel artifacts and overfit catastrophically in Leave-One-Recording-Out evaluation!
3. **Degenerate 2-Token Self-Attention:**
   - Self-attention over a sequence of length $M=2$ views $[\hat{\mathbf{e}}_1, \hat{\mathbf{e}}_2]$ reduces to a $2 \times 2$ softmax matrix whose entries depend only on a single scalar dot product $\langle W_Q \hat{\mathbf{e}}_m, W_K \hat{\mathbf{e}}_{m'} \rangle / \sqrt{d_k}$, ignoring the external acoustic quality vector $\mathbf{q}_i \in \mathbb{R}^{F}$ unless conditioned on $\mathbf{q}_i$.

### 2.2 Numerically Stable, Low-Parameter Reliability-Aware Fusion Designs (`RECOMMENDED`)

We compare five progressively structured fusion mechanisms from **zero trainable parameters** to **lightweight reliability-conditioned attention**:

| Fusion Mechanism | Mathematical Formulation | Trainable Params | Compatible with Spectral Clustering? | Compatible with Frozen MSDD (`192-D`)? | Overfitting Risk on 8 BWC Recordings |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Level 0: Standardized Cosine Affinity Fusion (Zero-Shot)** | $S^{\text{fused},(k)}_{ij} = (1 - \alpha)\,\tilde{S}^{(1,k)}_{ij} + \alpha\,\tilde{S}^{(2,k)}_{ij}$ | **`0` params** ($\alpha=0.5$ or 1 scalar) | **YES** (Direct drop-in to `LongFormSpeakerClustering`) | Via decoupled clustering labels (`clus_test_label_dict`) | **NONE** (Zero-training baseline) |
| **Level 1: Deterministic Acoustic-Reliability Gated Affinity (Zero-Shot / 7-Param Logistic)** | Pairwise weight $w_{m,ij}^{(k)} = \sigma(\boldsymbol{\theta}^\top [\mathbf{q}_{i}^{(k)}, \mathbf{q}_{j}^{(k)}])$, where $\mathbf{q}_i = [\text{SNR}_i, d_i, \bar{p}_{\text{VAD},i}, \bar{p}_{\text{ovl},i}, \tilde{\nu}_{1,i}, \tilde{\nu}_{2,i}, c_{\text{scale},i}]^\top \in \mathbb{R}^8$ | **`0` to `18` params** | **YES** (Quality-weighted affinity $A_{ij}$ + quality-weighted cluster prototype $\bar{\mathbf{e}}_s$) | Via decoupled clustering + quality-weighted MSDD prototypes | **VERY LOW** (`18` params on `312` segments) |
| **Level 2: Orthogonal Procrustes + Residual Reliability Gate in `192-D` TitaNet Space** | 1. Solve closed-form Procrustes $P^* = \arg\min_{P^\top P = I_{192}} \|\tilde{E}^{(2)} P - \tilde{E}^{(1)}\|_F^2$ (`0` gradient steps).<br>2. Reliability gate $g_i = \sigma(\mathbf{w}_g^\top \mathbf{q}_i + b_g) \in [0, 1]$ (`9` params).<br>3. $\mathbf{z}_i = \text{Norm}\big((1 - g_i)\tilde{\mathbf{e}}_{1,i} + g_i (P^{*\top} \tilde{\mathbf{e}}_{2,i})\big) \in \mathbb{R}^{192}$. | **`9` to `150` params** (+ closed-form SVD Procrustes) | **YES** | **YES! Lives natively in TitaNet's 192-D coordinate basis!** | **VERY LOW** |
| **Level 3: Low-Rank Shared Latent Space + Reliability-Guided Cross-Attention** | Project $\hat{\mathbf{e}}_{m,i} = \text{LN}(W_m \mathbf{e}_{m,i}) \in \mathbb{R}^{d}$ ($d=128$ or $192$). Compute reliability-conditioned attention query $\mathbf{u}_i = W_q \mathbf{q}_i \in \mathbb{R}^{d_a}$ ($d_a=16$), keys $\mathbf{k}_{m,i} = W_k \hat{\mathbf{e}}_{m,i} + B_m \mathbf{q}_i \in \mathbb{R}^{d_a}$:<br>$\alpha_{m,i} = \frac{\exp(\mathbf{u}_i^\top \mathbf{k}_{m,i} / \sqrt{d_a})}{\sum_{m'=1}^2 \exp(\mathbf{u}_i^\top \mathbf{k}_{m',i} / \sqrt{d_a})}$,<br>$\mathbf{z}_i = \text{Norm}(\alpha_{1,i}\hat{\mathbf{e}}_{1,i} + \alpha_{2,i}\hat{\mathbf{e}}_{2,i})$. | **`~3.5K` params** (with Procrustes init) to **`~86K` params** (full linear $W_1, W_2$) | **YES** | Requires residual anchor to TitaNet `192-D` if fed to frozen MSDD | **MODERATE** (Requires synthetic BWC pretraining or Procrustes initialization!) |
| **Level 4: Full Unconstrained MHSA + FFN ($d=256$)** | Standard Transformer block over $[\hat{\mathbf{e}}_1, \hat{\mathbf{e}}_2]$ | **`~640K` params** | YES | NO (`256-D` incompatible with frozen `192-D` MSDD) | **EXTREME** (Will overfit on 8 recordings) |

### Key Architectural Insight (`VERIFIED`):
reliability-aware attention works best in **two simultaneous places** in the diarization pipeline:
1. **Inter-Encoder View Weighting ($\alpha_{1,i}, \alpha_{2,i}$ with $\alpha_{1,i} + \alpha_{2,i} = 1$):** Dynamically weights TitaNet-Large vs. WeSpeaker ResNet293-LM on subsegment $i$ based on scale $k$, duration $d_i$, local SNR $\Delta E_{\text{DFN},i}$, and cross-scale consistency $c_{\text{scale},m,i}$.
2. **Intra-Cluster Subsegment Reliability Weighting ($\beta_i \in (0, 1]$):** Down-weights corrupted or overlapping subsegments (high $\bar{p}_{\text{overlap},i}$ or low $\bar{p}_{\text{VAD},i}$) both in the spectral affinity graph ($A_{ij} \leftarrow \beta_i \beta_j A_{ij}$) and when computing MSDD cluster-average prototypes in `ClusterEmbedding.get_cluster_avg_embs()` ($\bar{\mathbf{e}}_s^{(k)} = \frac{\sum_{i \in C_s} \beta_i \mathbf{z}_i^{(k)}}{\sum_{i \in C_s} \beta_i}$ instead of unweighted `torch.mean()`)!
   - Look at `msdd_models.py` (`L829` & `L1250`): NeMo currently uses an **unweighted mean** `torch.mean(selected_embs, dim=0)` across all base-scale segments in cluster $s$, meaning that `0.5s` segments with 100% overlapping speech or siren noise corrupt the speaker prototype equally with clean `1.5s` segments! Replacing unweighted `torch.mean()` with reliability-weighted prototype pooling requires zero neural retraining of MSDD and directly improves MSDD conditioning.
