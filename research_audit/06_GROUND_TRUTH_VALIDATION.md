## 2. Hardcoded `bwc`-Specific Curation in `step00_ground_truth.py` (`VERIFIED`)

Inspecting [`phase20_standalone/scripts/step00_ground_truth.py`](file:///home/priyanka/chaitanya/Multi-View-Speaker-Representation-Learning/phase20_standalone/scripts/step00_ground_truth.py#L38-L72) reveals `apply_legacy_reference_curation(segments)`, which is called unconditionally on **every** recording unless `--disable-legacy-curation` is passed (`L137–138`):
- It checks for exact `(speaker, start, end)` tuples from `bwc.txt` (e.g., `Speaker_2` at `19.0–20.0`, `Speaker_1` at `134.0–135.0`, `Speaker_2` at `135.0–138.0`, `Speaker_2` at `141.0–145.0`) and merges/mutates them to reduce `bwc.txt` from **54 raw segments (503 words)** to **48 curated segments (484 words)** in `ground_truth_json/bwc.json` and **37 positive-duration RTTM segments** in `ground_truth_rttm/bwc.rttm`.
- Because it matches on `(speaker, start, end)` tuples without checking `recording == "bwc"`, any other recording that happens to have `Speaker_2` at `[00:00:19 - 00:00:20]` would have its ground truth silently mutated unless `--disable-legacy-curation` is specified!

## 3. Impact of 1-Second Integer Timestamp Quantization on Diarization & Multi-View Learning (`Phase D5`)

All 12 ground-truth files in `ground_truth/` use whole-second `HH:MM:SS` timestamps (`±0.5s` quantization error at both onset and offset, up to `±1.0s` error on duration):

1. **Why `collar = 0.0` DER Is Artificially Inflated (`VERIFIED`):**
   - On `bwc`, evaluating the exact same MSDD output at `collar = 0.0, skip_overlap = False` gives **`DER = 0.4471`**, whereas `collar = 0.25, skip_overlap = True` gives **`DER = 0.3241`** (`12.3%` absolute DER difference solely from `0.25s` boundary collar and overlap skipping!).
   - Furthermore, because `120 / 432` (`27.8%`) of all annotated turns across the 8 audio recordings have `start == end` (`0.0s` duration in RTTM), whenever a diarization system correctly detects a 0.6-second backchannel (`"Yeah"`, `"No"`, `"Wait"`) that was rounded to `[00:00:28 - 00:00:28]` in the ground truth, **Pyannote's DER metric penalizes that true speech detection as False Alarm (`FA`)!**
2. **Impact on Extracting Ground-Truth Speaker Segments for Multi-View Training (`Phase D5`):**
   - If you slice audio directly using raw `[start, end]` intervals from `ground_truth/*.txt`:
     - All 120 zero-duration turns (`start == end`) yield empty `0.0s` audio slices.
     - Integer-rounded boundaries `[start, end]` frequently include up to `0.5s` of adjacent speaker speech or silence at segment edges.
     - In `Dennis Skinner` (`sum_pos = 284s` vs `176.3s` audio) and `Piers Confronts Tommy Robinson` (`sum_pos = 284s` vs `204.6s` audio), over 50% of the segments contain heavy multi-speaker overlap (debating/shouting over each other), which contaminates single-speaker embedding supervision if overlapping intervals are not subtracted!
   - **Mandatory Remediation for Multi-View Representation Evaluation/Training:**
     1. Fix the 16 Unicode en-dashes `–` and `0:02:25` in `Piers Confronts Tommy Robinson.txt` (in a non-destructive preprocessing loader).
     2. Exclude or subtract overlapping ground-truth intervals when extracting clean single-speaker reference prototypes.
     3. Intersect integer-rounded GT intervals `[start, end]` with Path A VAD intervals (or Wav2Vec2 word-aligned boundaries) to trim leading/trailing non-speech and recover sub-second turns around `start == end` timestamps (`[t - 0.25s, t + 0.25s]` only for evaluation diagnostics, never for clean prototype training).
